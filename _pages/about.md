My name is Siyuan Song (宋思远). I am a Ph.D. student in Psychology at [Princeton University](https://psych.princeton.edu/), advised by Dr. [Brenden Lake](https://psych.princeton.edu/people/brenden-lake) and Dr. [Adele Goldberg](https://psych.princeton.edu/people/adele-goldberg).

Before joining Princeton, I received my B.A. in Linguistics with Special Honors and Highest Honors from [UT Linguistics](https://liberalarts.utexas.edu/linguistics/), along with a Certificate in Applied Statistical Modeling. I was a member of the [UT CompLing Group](https://sites.utexas.edu/compling/) and the [UT NLP community](https://www.nlp.utexas.edu/), where I was fortunate to be advised by Dr. [Kyle Mahowald](https://mahowak.github.io/) and Dr. [Kanishka Misra](https://kanishka.website/).

I visited [TedLab at MIT BCS](https://tedlab.mit.edu/) in summer 2025, mentored by [Thomas Clark](https://thomashikaru.github.io/), and worked as a Visiting Undergraduate Research Intern at [CoCoDev at Harvard](https://www.cocodev.org/) in summer 2024, mentored by Dr. [Jennifer Hu](https://jennhu.github.io/).

Before transferring to UT in Fall 2024, I studied at the [School of Foreign Languages at SJTU](https://sfl.sjtu.edu.cn/en/). There, I began working in computational linguistics under the guidance of Dr. [Hai Hu](https://huhailinguist.github.io/) and have continued collaborating with his [CL Lab](https://huhailinguist.github.io/lab/).


In general, my research aims to:

- Use computational models to study the mechanisms underlying first- and second-language acquisition and language processing.

- Study the roles of multimodal input and multi-agent interaction in language learning.

- Evaluate language models using scientific and cognitively motivated methods.

- Build data-efficient AI systems that learn and reason more like humans.

In my spare time, I make [music]({{ site.baseurl }}/music/), play and watch sports, and cook.
